Privacy Policy


J Bogdan Consulting LLC has created this privacy statement to demonstrate our commitment to privacy.  J Bogdan Consulting LLC is committed to respecting and protecting your right to privacy and takes your privacy seriously.  We have privacy policies and we strive to keep your personal and financial information secure.  To that end, we have prepared this privacy policy to disclose and describe our information gathering and dissemination practices for this website.  This privacy policy does not govern privacy practices associated with offline activities of J Bogdan Consulting LLC.

Most of all, we value your privacy.  We do not sell, rent, loan, or give your email address or other personal information to anyone and will never do so.

Your Internet Protocol (IP) Address


Every computer connected to the Internet is given a domain name and a set of numbers that serve as that computer's "Internet Protocol" IP address.  When a visitor requests a page from any website within the J Bogdan Consulting LLC network, our Web servers automatically recognize that visitor's domain name and IP address.  We use this information to examine our traffic in aggregate, and to investigate misuse of the J Bogdan Consulting LLC Network, its users, or to cooperate with law enforcement.

Use of Cookies


From time to time J Bogdan Consulting LLC may send a "cookie" to your computer.  A cookie is a small piece of data that is sent to your Internet browser from a Web server and stored on your computer's hard drive.  A cookie cannot read data from your computer hard disk or read cookie files created by other Web sites.  Cookies do not damage your system.  We use cookies to identify which areas of J Bogdan Consulting LLC's Network you have visited or customized, so the next time you visit, those pages may be readily accessible.  You can choose whether to accept cookies by changing the settings of your Internet browser.  You can reset your browser to refuse all cookies, or allow your browser to show you when a cookie is being sent.  If you choose not to accept these cookies, your experience at our Site and other Web sites may be diminished and some features may not work as intended.

Single-pixel gifs


J Bogdan Consulting LLC may use single-pixel gif images, sometimes referred to as web bugs or web beacons, to count Web page accesses and gather some general statistical information.

Registration Forms


J Bogdan Consulting LLC offers access to some services that require registration.  We may use your business contact information to send notification to you of J Bogdan Consulting LLC products and services by e-mail and/or postal mail.  We do not share this information with any third party.

Information Collected when Making a Purchase


If you are purchasing something from J Bogdan Consulting LLC, we need to know your name, e-mail address, mailing address, credit card number, and expiration date.  This allows us to process and fill your order and to notify you of your order status.  We may also use this business contact information to notify you of related products and services.  Credit card and e-mail information will not be shared or sold to third parties for any purpose except as provided in this Privacy Policy without your express permission.

On selected pages, J Bogdan Consulting LLC uses the industry standard SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encrypted servers.  SSL encrypts the information transferred between your browser and our servers, rendering it unreadable to anyone trying to intercept the information.
